Leads in. Qualified, deduplicated and in the CRM out.

Website forms, emails and booked meetings arrive in an n8n workflow. A Python service checks who the lead already is, reads their company's website, scores and routes them with an LLM whose answer is checked against a schema and overruled by rules in code, then writes the contact, company, deal and a note explaining the score to HubSpot. Hot leads ping Slack. Unsure ones wait here for a person.

  1. Webhook in n8nForm, inbound email (Postmark) or booking (Cal.com), signatures checked.
  2. Stored onceA redelivered webhook maps to the same lead.
  3. QualifiedDedupe, website enrichment (robots.txt respected), LLM score, rules.
  4. Reviewed if unsureLow confidence, duplicates or odd messages wait for a person.
  5. SyncedHubSpot contact, company, deal and note; Slack alert for hot leads.
